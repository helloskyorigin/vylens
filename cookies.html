The cookie situation, explained.

A plain-language note on browser storage, third parties and tracking on this site.

Current website: no first-party analytics or advertising trackers are installed in the published frontend code.

What this site uses today

The current VYLENS code does not set its own advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. The site uses sessionStorage to carry audit details between pages and localStorage to remember your light or dark theme choice. Product files are temporarily placed in browser IndexedDB for page-to-page transfer, then removed when the inspection page retrieves them. These are functional browser-storage mechanisms, not advertising cookies. We have not added a cookie-consent banner because the site currently does not intentionally run nonessential tracking cookies.

Third-party requests

Google Fonts may receive network requests when your browser loads fonts; the PDF text reader is also loaded from the cdnjs/Cloudflare CDN when you inspect a PDF. The PDF file itself is not sent to that CDN by VYLENS code. Your hosting provider may also process technical access logs and may set its own security-related cookies if its infrastructure requires them. We cannot promise there are no cookies placed by external service infrastructure outside this codebase.

Your controls

You can block or delete cookies using your browser settings, and you can block external fonts using suitable privacy controls. Some styling or features may look different if resources are blocked.

Future changes

If VYLENS adds login sessions, analytics, advertising integrations or any nonessential cookies, we will update this explanation and implement any consent or notice controls required by applicable law.
