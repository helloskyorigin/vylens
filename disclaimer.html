Accuracy first. VYLENS avoids suggesting that a hypothetical sample report is a real analysis of your website or files.

Sample reports are illustrations

The sample findings displayed on the VYLENS landing page use fictional examples. They are not results from scans of a visitor’s actual website or digital product. Clicking a sample report shows the design and nature of the proposed feedback, not the output of a live inspection.

Audits cannot explain every outcome

A website or product-file review can reveal observable presentation, usability, content and offer problems, but cannot prove why visitors did not buy or determine all external factors affecting revenue. Advice is not a guarantee of a particular business outcome, ranking, conversion rate or profit.

Professional and security limitations

VYLENS is intended as product-improvement feedback, not as penetration testing, compliance certification, a legal opinion or a formal accessibility certification. For high-stakes or regulated matters, use an appropriately qualified professional.

Service availability

Live AI auditing, report delivery and payments are still being connected. An enquiry email is not an order confirmation and does not authorize a charge.
