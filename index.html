GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

FAQ.

Everything you need before starting a scan.

What does VYLENS actually check?+

For websites, VYLENS reviews offer clarity, value, trust signals and buying steps using the pages it can inspect. It also checks mobile issues and keeps supporting observations in the PDF. A scan can review up to 24 discovered pages, but some pages may be blocked or unavailable.

Why might customers hesitate to buy?+

An unclear benefit, missing product details, weak proof, confusing prices or a difficult purchase step can raise questions. Your report explains concerns supported by the website, rather than guessing what individual visitors thought.

What will I get after a scan?+

Five buyer signals and a concise report covering possible blockers, buyer questions, exact fixes, useful rewrites when supported, and an action plan. You can download the full PDF. If the site cannot be inspected, VYLENS will explain why instead of making up results.

Can I audit a digital product too?+

Yes. Upload a supported PDF, image, text file or ZIP. VYLENS reviews the file in your browser. You can also add a sales-page link for extra offer context. It will not edit or publish your product.

Is it free, and do I need an account?+

Current audits and the full report are free. No signup or payment card is required. Need help? Email VYLENS.
