Who we are

VYLENS inspects discoverable public website pages using bounded Playwright-rendered DOM/mobile checks and HTML checks, and checks supported digital-product files locally. For privacy questions or requests, email hello.skyorigin@gmail.com. This page describes what the currently published site does; it will be updated before server-side file uploads, AI processing or checkout are enabled.

What you enter

You may submit a public website URL for an automated audit. The URL is sent to the VYLENS server function, which discovers and fetches a bounded set of linked public pages and sitemap entries and uses a browser to inspect rendered DOM and mobile layout where possible. Related public assets may also be requested, including externally hosted resources needed for rendering. Website and resource hosts may receive server requests and retain access logs. For a digital product, the chosen file passes between the landing and inspection pages through temporary browser storage and is read locally, then removed from that temporary transfer store after retrieval. File bytes are not uploaded to VYLENS. If you provide an optional public sales-page URL, that URL is sent to the same VYLENS website-inspection function to check its public HTML; the selected product file and the price field remain in your browser session and are not uploaded to that function. Website analyses can be incomplete due to time and page limits, robots rules, blocking, inaccessible pages or JavaScript behavior; the report records partial coverage. Forms are not intentionally submitted, and private pages are not inspected.

Emails you send us

If you contact us, the content you choose to send—including email address, your message, links or attachments—is received through our email provider and used to review and respond to your request. Please avoid emailing passwords, payment credentials, confidential customer data or sensitive information unless strictly necessary. Ask before sharing private documents.

Hosting and third-party services

The website may be hosted by a third-party provider that processes standard request information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps and error logs for operation and security. The site requests typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive connection data such as IP address. For PDF inspection, the browser may download the PDF.js reader library from cdnjs/Cloudflare or jsDelivr; this requests library code, not the contents of your selected PDF. Third-party services have their own policies. We do not currently embed an advertising pixel or an analytics tracker in this codebase.

Retention and sharing

Website inspection requests are processed during the request without intentionally storing the fetched HTML or report in a VYLENS database. Browser session data remains in your browser; selected files are removed from temporary file-transfer storage when the inspection page retrieves them (abandoned transfers may remain until overwritten or cleared in site storage). Hosting services may retain standard access/error logs. We do not currently run a file-upload audit backend. Emails and attachments are retained in our mailbox as needed to handle the conversation, legitimate business recordkeeping or legal requests; retention depends in part on the email provider. We do not publish your private documents or sell your email message contents to advertisers. If you want an email or attachment deleted, send us a request and we will assess it subject to applicable obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can decline to send an email or attachment, close this page, and choose not to select any file. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, or to raise a privacy concern. Applicable privacy-law rights depend on your location and the nature of the data. We will handle requests under the law that applies.

Security and updates

Avoid sending private files over email unless you are comfortable with that channel. The current browser-assisted website inspector is an early feature and has not been independently certified. Please avoid submitting non-public internal URLs or passwords. This policy will be updated before live AI processing, accounts, payments or file storage are introduced. Material policy changes will be shown with a new updated date.

Need clarification?

We'd rather answer your question than leave you guessing. Contact VYLENS directly.
