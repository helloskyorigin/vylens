Who we are

VYLENS is a digital audit product in development, offering website and digital-product review concepts. For privacy questions or requests, email hello.skyorigin@gmail.com. This page describes what the currently published site does; it will be revised before live uploads, AI processing and checkout are enabled.

What you enter

The currently published landing page lets you type a website URL, optionally type a sales-page link or price, and select a product file. Those inputs are handled within your browser by page JavaScript. The site currently does not transmit them to an audit API or upload files to VYLENS. The selected filename may appear on screen. If you choose to open an email request, your browser passes the text of your request to your email application; product files are not automatically attached.

Emails you send us

If you contact us, the content you choose to send—including email address, your message, links or attachments—is received through our email provider and used to review and respond to your request. Please avoid emailing passwords, payment credentials, confidential customer data or sensitive information unless strictly necessary. Ask before sharing private documents.

Hosting and third-party services

The website may be hosted by a third-party provider that processes standard request information such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps and error logs for operation and security. The site requests typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive connection data such as IP address. Third-party services have their own policies. We do not currently embed an advertising pixel or an analytics tracker in this codebase.

Retention and sharing

Inputs that remain in your browser are not retained by a VYLENS audit backend because no such backend is connected. Emails and attachments are retained in our mailbox as needed to handle the conversation, legitimate business recordkeeping or legal requests; retention depends in part on the email provider. We do not publish your private documents or sell your email message contents to advertisers. If you want an email or attachment deleted, send us a request and we will assess it subject to applicable obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can decline to send an email or attachment, close this page, and choose not to select any file. You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, or to raise a privacy concern. Applicable privacy-law rights depend on your location and the nature of the data. We will handle requests under the law that applies.

Security and updates

Avoid sending private files over email unless you are comfortable with that channel. We cannot claim that a future audit-processing pipeline has been audited or certified before it exists. This policy will be updated before live AI processing, accounts, payments or file storage are introduced. Material policy changes will be shown with a new updated date.

Need clarification?

We'd rather answer your question than leave you guessing. Contact VYLENS directly.
