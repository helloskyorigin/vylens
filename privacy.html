This page describes the current demonstration frontend. The privacy policy will be updated before live file uploads, AI processing or payments are enabled.

Information entered on this page

Website addresses, selected filenames, optional product details and form state are used only within your browser during the current session. Selecting a file does not upload it to VYLENS in this preview.

Before a real audit launch
